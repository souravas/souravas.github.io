# /exercises

- Each exercise in [index.html](index.html) is a link to its YouTube video, opened in the same tab as on /start, holding its thumbnail (`i.ytimg.com/vi_webp/<id>/sddefault.webp`, which the CSP allows in `img-src`). Keep `&t=` off the links, so every video starts at the beginning.
- The thumbnails sit two across on phones and three from 760px, so all five fit on one screen.
