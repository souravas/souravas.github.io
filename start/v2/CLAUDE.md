# /start/v2 — Keypad

/start's default design since November 2026 (see [../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md), *The default*): every bookmark a key, each group a block of keys under its name, the blocks closing into one rectangle sized to the window. The build copies this page to /start, so a change here shows at both addresses. [index.html](index.html) is only a head and a `<!--bookmarks-->` marker, which the `start-bookmarks` plugin fills with [../bookmarks.html](../bookmarks.html), so **bookmarks are changed there, never here**. [main.js](main.js) imports /start's prompt (`../prompt.js`) and registers `/sw.js` with the scope of the address it was opened at (`/start/v2`, or `/start` for the copy), which serves the page from cache at once. Only [style.css](style.css) is this page's own.

- **Legends**: a key prints its short code (`data-alias`) top left and, when it opens several tabs, their count top right, from `data-tabs`, which the plugin adds (the URLs in `data-also` plus the href). Both are CSS `content` with empty alt text, so screen readers read only the name.
- **Layout**: one grid of key columns (`--cols`). A group takes as many columns as it sets keys to a row: `--six` for a group of six links (any group with a fifth link), `--four` for any smaller one (Prep's two and Fun's three count as four). The arrangement follows the window's shape:

  | Window | Columns | Rows of groups |
  |---|---|---|
  | under 360px | 2 | each group a row of its own |
  | phones, and from 760px windows narrower than 5:6 | 4 | Daily Code / Design Learn / Prep Fun, groups of six two keys wide and three deep |
  | from 760px and 5:6 or wider | 8 | Daily Code Design Learn (two keys wide, three deep) / Prep Fun (each one row of four) |
  | from 1500px and 3:1 or wider | 16 | all six in one row: groups of six three wide and two deep, Prep and Fun two by two |

  **The arrangements count on four groups of six, then two of at most four** (32 places: 4 × 8, 8 × 4, 16 × 2). Before November 2026 there were seven groups and 36 keys, in 6, 9 and 12 columns; with 32 places those leave a hole. When Finance went that month and its Cashflow joined Daily, Prep had four links and the groups closed into a rectangle in every arrangement, Fun's fourth place the only empty one. Then LLD and Frontend went and Design Patterns moved to Design, leaving Prep two, so its last two places are empty too: the end of its row in eight columns, its second row in four and sixteen. Two more links in Prep (and one in Fun) close the rectangle again; another group, or a fifth link in Prep or Fun, leaves holes elsewhere, so look at every arrangement after changing the groups. The ratios are where the next arrangement starts to give larger keys.
- **Fit**: from 760px the page is sized to the window and centred. `--key`, a key's height, is the largest at which the arrangement fits both ways, up to 160px, and the gaps, icons and type all follow it. The formula's numbers come from the gap ratios, the tallest the prompt and a heading get (47px and 24px), and `--across`, how many keys wide the arrangement's widest row of groups is, gaps and padding included (5.05, 9.75, 18.85), as the comment above it in style.css spells out: change one and the other has to follow, or the page stops fitting. A key is between square and a quarter wider than tall. On a maximised window the page fills about three quarters of the area: 2560 × 945px gives eight columns of 190 × 152px keys, 1536 × 725px eight of 141 × 113px.
- **Smallest**: a key goes down to 80px, the least that holds a name on two lines (the second runs into the key's lower padding); names wrap in keys under about 97px wide. Half a laptop's screen (768 × 725px) gets eight columns of 79 × 80px keys, so the page fits there. In four columns from 760px, only in tall windows where keys are wide, it goes to 72px. "AlgoMonster", the longest word, needs a key about 76px wide with the key's 2px side padding (it was 4px until the eight-column arrangement made keys that narrow); from 760px every key is wider.
